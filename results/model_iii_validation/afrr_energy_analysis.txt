CRITICAL INTERPRETATION:
========================

WHY ARE THERE BIDS WITH ZERO PRICES?
-------------------------------------

Price = 0 in aFRR energy market means "MARKET NOT ACTIVATED"
This is NOT the same as "free energy at zero price"

The preprocessing correctly handles this:
  1. Original data: price = 0 when market inactive
  2. Preprocessing: 0 -> NaN (prevents false arbitrage)
  3. Optimization: Uses NaN data (no bids placed)
  4. Visualization: Shows original prices (including 0s)
